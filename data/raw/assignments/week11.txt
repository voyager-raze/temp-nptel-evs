QUESTION 1:

Assertion (A):
Compounds having high log Kow values tend to bioaccumulate strongly.
Reason (R):
Such compounds are more soluble in organic solvents than in water.

    a. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    b. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
    c. A is true but R is false
    d. A is false but R is true

Detailed Solution: High log Kow values indicate that a chemical is more soluble in fats and
organic matter than in water. Such compounds preferentially accumulate in biological tissues,
resulting in higher bioaccumulation potential.

QUESTION 5:

b. Flood control
c. Nutrient recycling
d. Disease regulation

Detailed Solution:

Climate regulation, flood control, and disease regulation help maintain environmental balance

QUESTION 7:

The concentration of a pesticide in fish tissue is measured as 3.6 mg/kg. The concentration of
the same pesticide in surrounding water is 0.00012 mg/L. The Bioconcentration Factor (BCF)
is:

    a. 3 × 10³ L/kg
    b. 3 × 10 L/kg
    c. 300 L/kg
    d. 30 L/kg

Detailed Solution:

BCF = 3.6/ 0.00012 = 30000
BCF = 3×104 L/kg

Solution: A BCF of 30,000 L/kg indicates strong uptake of the contaminant from water into fish
tissues. Higher BCF values imply greater potential for bioaccumulation.

QUESTION 11:

    a. 875 kcal m² yr¹
    b. 1625 kcal m² yr¹
    c. 2150 kcal m² yr¹
    d. 2500 kcal m² yr¹
Detailed Solution:
Given: GPP = 2500 kcal m² yr¹
Respiration = 35%
R=0.35×2500=875
NPP=GPP-R=2500-875=1625 kcal m² yr¹

QUESTION 17:

Assertion (A):

Overgrazing accelerates soil erosion.

Reason (R):

Excessive grazing removes vegetation cover and reduces soil cohesion.

    a. Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A
    b. Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation
    c. A is true but R is false
    d. A is false but R is true

Detailed Solution: Overgrazing removes protective vegetation and weakens root binding of
soil particles. The exposed soil becomes vulnerable to runoff and wind action, leading to
increased soil erosion. Hence both A and R are true, and R correctly explains A.
