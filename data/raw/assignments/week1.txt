1. A chemical is found in a river at concentrations above natural background levels. No
    adverse ecological effects are observed. The chemical is best classified as:
    a) Pollutant only
    b) Contaminant only
    c) Both pollutant and contaminant
    d) Neither

Detailed Solution: Contamination means the compound is present at concentrations above
background level; Pollution means the compound causes adverse effect on human or
ecological systems.

2. According to the Brundtland Report, sustainable development requires:
    a) Meeting present needs without compromising future generations needs
    b) Unlimited resource extraction for economic growth
    c) Balancing environmental protection with quality-of-life improvement
    d) Long-term protection of environmental systems

Detailed Solution: Development should satisfy current needs while preserving the ability of
future generations to satisfy theirs, that means-Present generation must benefit (quality of
life); Future generations must not suffer; and Environment must be protected
Option B violates sustainability because unlimited extraction leads to resource depletion.
3. "A researcher obtains the following R² values while fitting population data:

    Model A: 0.32
    Model B: 0.71
    Model C: 0.96
    Which model best describes the observed data?"
    a) Model A
    b) Model B
    c) Model C
    d) Cannot be determined
Detailed Solution: R² measures goodness of fit. R2 >0.9, it is a reasonably good fit and >0.95 is
a very good fit. Values of R2 <0.5 show that there is no correlation between the two
independent variables. The two independent variables in this example are population and
time.
4. If a class has a capacity for 100 students now, and the capacity for students is to be

    increased by 10% every year, what will the student intake capacity be at the end of 30
    years?
    a) 2718
    b) 2008
    c) 1500
    d) 3000
Detailed Solution: As P0 = 100, n = number of years = 30 and r = 10% or 0.1
P=P0*exp (n*r) = 100*exp (30*0.1) = 2008
5. "A country successfully reduces its population growth rate but doubles its per-capita
    resource consumption. What is the most likely outcome?"
    a) Environmental pressure will certainly decrease.
    b) Environmental pressure may still increase.
    c) Resource consumption becomes irrelevant.
    d) Pollution must decrease.
Detailed Solution: Environmental impact depends on: Population × Consumption ×
Technology
So, if population growth decreases, a large increase in consumption can offset the gains.
This is why some low-population countries still have very high environmental footprints.
6. Which of the following are correctly classified as renewable resources?
    a) Air
    b) Soil
    c) Coal
    d) Water
Detailed Solution: renewable resource can naturally restore within a reasonable time scale.
Air is continuously renewed through atmospheric processes. Water is renewed through the
hydrologic cycle. Soil is renewable, although very slowly. Coal requires millions of years to
form and is therefore non-renewable.
7. Organic matter discharged into a river is decomposed/degraded naturally. This ability or
    self-purification capacity of a river is an example of:
    a) Natural resource
    b) Natural service
    c) Non-renewable resource
    d) Green design

Detailed Solution: The river is performing an ecological function. The water itself is a resource.
Its ability to assimilate and degrade wastes is a service provided by nature.
Therefore this is a natural service.

9. "Two buildings use identical construction materials.
    Building X minimizes energy use only during operation.
    Building Y minimizes energy and resource use throughout manufacturing, operation,
    maintenance, and disposal.
    Which is more consistent with sustainable design?"
    a) Building X
    b) Building Y
    c) Both equally
    d) Neither
Detailed Solution: Green design mostly focuses on reducing current environmental impacts.
Sustainable design considers the entire lifecycle: Manufacturing, Transportation, Operation,
Maintenance, Disposal. Therefore, Building Y represents sustainable design.

10. What is the fundamental weakness of a linear economy?
    a) Resources are endlessly recycled.
    b) Waste generation is minimized.
    c) Resources move in a one-way flow from extraction to disposal.
    d) Renewable energy dominates production.
Detailed Solution: Linear Economy: Take  Make  Dispose
Resources are extracted, converted into products, and discarded. This model ignores material
recovery.
11. Which of the following changes would move a city from a Petropolis toward an Ecopolis?
    a) Increasing local food production
    b) Expanding waste recycling
    c) Increasing fossil-fuel imports
    d) Promoting renewable energy
Detailed Solution: An Ecopolis: Reduce external dependence, minimize waste, Close material
loops, use renewable resources so, Local production, Recycling and Renewable energy
support the transition. Increasing fossil-fuel imports does the opposite.
12. If a country doubles its electricity generation using coal while maintaining the same
    population. What is the most likely environmental consequence?
    a) Reduced greenhouse gas emissions
    b) Increased resource consumption and pollution
    c) No environmental change
    d) Reduced dependence on natural resources
Detailed Solution: Coal: Non-renewable and Carbon intensive Doubling coal-based generation
implies: More coal extraction More combustion More CO emissions More air
pollution Greater environmental burden. Thus, environmental impacts increase even
without population growth.
13. The Capital Stock Model suggests that sustainable development is achieved when:
    a) Capital stock is continuously depleted
    b) Natural resources are exhausted
    c) Society lives on interest rather than capital
    d) Population is reduced to zero
Detailed Solution: The lecture compares sustainability to living on the interest generated by
capital rather than consuming the capital itself. Consuming the capital stock threatens long-
term prosperity.

17. Earth Overshoot Day represents:
    a) The day Earth begins regeneration
    b) The day humanity's resource demand exceeds Earth's annual regeneration capacity
    c) The first day of the year
    d) The day carbon emissions become zero
Detailed Solution: Earth Overshoot Day is the date when humanity has consumed all
ecological resources that Earth can regenerate during that year. After this day, humanity is
operating on an ecological deficit.

19. Factors affecting EPI include:

    a) Number of occupants
    b) Climate and Seasonal Variation
    c) Energy conservation practices
    d) Shape and building orientation
Detailed Solution:
Number of Occupants: More occupants generally increase lighting demand, cooling load,
appliance usage.
Climate and Seasonal Variations: Hot climates increase air-conditioning demand, energy
consumption. Cold climates increase heating demand.
Energy Conservation Practices: Efficient lighting, sensors, and renewable energy reduce EPI.
Shape and Orientation: Building orientation affects sunlight exposure, ventilation, cooling
requirements.
