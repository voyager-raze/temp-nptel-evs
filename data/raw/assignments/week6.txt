QUESTION 1:

Which of the following geothermal energy technologies involves creating engineered
underground reservoirs by enhancing or artificially stimulating rock formations, thereby
enabling the extraction of geothermal energy from locations that would otherwise be
inaccessible?

a. Dry steam power plants
b. Flash steam power plants
c. Binary cycle power plants
d. Enhanced geothermal systems

Correct answer: d. Enhanced geothermal systems

Solution: Refer to Slide #16 of Lecture #1. Enhanced geothermal systems create artificial
underground reservoirs by injecting fluid into hot, impermeable rock formations to improve
permeability and heat extraction. This allows geothermal energy to be harnessed from
regions where natural geothermal reservoirs are absent or inaccessible.

      a. 3.4 GW
      b. 34 GW
      c. 340 GW
      d. 0.34 GW

Correct answer: a. 3.4 GW

The average power (Pavg) harvested from a tidal basin is (.g.A.h2) / (2T)
Given:  = 1000 kg m3, g = 9.81 m s2, A = 470 × 106 m2, h = 8 m, T = 12 × 3600 s
Substituting, Pavg = (1000 × 9.81 × 470 × 106 × 82) / (2 × 12 × 3600)

                       = 3.42 × 109 W = 3.4 GW
QUESTION 3:

Which of the following ocean energy technologies has the largest estimated global
technical energy potential?

      a. Tidal energy
      b. Wave energy
      c. Ocean thermal energy conversion
      d. Salinity gradient energy

Correct answer: c. OTEC
Solution: Refer to Slide #5 of Lecture #3. OTEC exploits the temperature difference
between warm surface water and cold deep ocean water. Because tropical oceans cover
vast areas and operate continuously, OTEC has the largest estimated global technical
potential, often exceeding 44,000 TWh per year, which is significantly greater than the
estimated potentials of tidal, wave, or salinity-gradient energy technologies.

QUESTION 4:

Assertion (A): Fast pyrolysis produces bio-oil by rapidly heating biomass in the absence
of oxygen.

Reason (R): The absence of oxygen prevents combustion and enables the thermal
decomposition of biomass into liquid products.

      a. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.
      b. Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
      c. A is true, but R is false.
      d. A is false, but R is true.

Correct answer: a. Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.

Solution: Refer to Slide #13 of Lecture #5. Fast pyrolysis involves heating biomass rapidly
in an oxygen-free environment. The absence of oxygen prevents the biomass from
burning and instead promotes thermal decomposition, producing bio-oil as the major
product along with biochar and syngas. Therefore, the reason correctly explains the
assertion.

Point absorbers and attenuators are classified as oscillating-body wave energy
converters because they primarily

     a. convert pressure waves propagating through a flexible, water-filled tube into
          electrical energy.

     b. convert the oscillatory motion of floating or submerged structures induced by
          ocean waves into useful power.

     c. generate electricity from bidirectional airflow produced by the oscillation of a
          water column.

          wave overtopping.

Correct answer(s): b. convert the oscillatory motion of floating or submerged

Solution: Refer to Slide #18 of Lecture #2. Point absorbers and attenuators generate
electricity by converting the oscillatory motion of floating or submerged structures induced
by ocean waves into useful power. This wave-induced body motion is the defining feature
of oscillating-body wave energy converters.

      c. 207 GW
      d. 207 W

Solution: Refer to Slide #9 of Lecture #4.

Therefore, Pelec = ehydromwg(z) = 0.77 × 500,000 × 9.81 × 55 = 2.07 × 108 W = 207 MW
QUESTION 7:

In the anaerobic digestion of food waste, which stage involves the breakdown of complex
organic polymers such as carbohydrates, proteins, and lipids into simpler soluble
compounds that can be utilized by microorganisms?

      a. Acidogenesis
      b. Acetogenesis
      c. Hydrolysis
      d. Methanogenesis

Correct answer: c. Hydrolysis
Solution: Hydrolysis is the first stage of anaerobic digestion, during which complex
organic matter is converted into simpler soluble molecules such as sugars, amino acids,
and fatty acids, making them available for subsequent microbial degradation.

QUESTION 9:

Wave energy is often described as an indirect form of solar energy because

      a. Solar heating of the Earth's surface generates winds that transfer energy to
          ocean waves.

      b. Solar radiation directly heats ocean water, producing surface waves.
      c. Wave motion is primarily caused by the thermal expansion of seawater.
      d. Ocean waves are generated by tidal forces induced by solar radiation.

Correct answer: a. Solar heating of the Earth's surface generates winds that transfer
energy to ocean waves.
Solution: Refer to Slide #11 of Lecture #2. Most ocean waves are generated by wind.
Since winds are driven by the uneven heating of the Earth's surface by the Sun, wave
energy is considered an indirect form of solar energy.

QUESTION 10:

Which of the following characteristics distinguishes geothermal energy from many other
renewable energy resources?

      a. It is available only during daylight hours.
      b. It can provide reliable, continuous baseload power.
      c. Its output is directly governed by seasonal weather conditions.
      d. It relies on large-scale energy storage to ensure a stable power supply.

Correct answer: b. It can provide reliable, continuous baseload power.
Solution: Unlike many renewable energy sources that are influenced by weather
conditions, geothermal energy can provide a continuous and reliable supply of power,
making it well-suited for baseload electricity generation.

QUESTION 11:

Which of the following are potential benefits of biomass pretreatment intended for biofuel
production?

a. Increasing the surface area available for enzymatic hydrolysis.
b. Reducing cellulose crystallinity and improving enzyme accessibility.
c. Disrupting the lignin­carbohydrate matrix to expose fermentable

    polysaccharides.
d. Directly converting cellulose and hemicellulose into biofuels during the

    pretreatment step.

Correct answer(s):

     a. Increasing the surface area available for enzymatic hydrolysis.
     b. Reducing cellulose crystallinity and improving enzyme accessibility.
     c. Disrupting the lignin­carbohydrate matrix to expose fermentable

          polysaccharides.

Solution: Refer to Slide #11 of Lecture #5. The primary goals of biomass pretreatment
are to increase enzyme accessibility by modifying the physical and chemical structure of
lignocellulosic biomass. Pretreatment facilitates subsequent hydrolysis and fermentation
but does not directly convert biomass into biofuels.
QUESTION 12:

Which of the following environmental impacts are commonly associated with the
construction and operation of large hydroelectric dams?

      a. Alteration of natural river flow patterns.
      b. Disruption of fish migration and aquatic ecosystems.
     c. Submergence of terrestrial habitats due to reservoir formation.
     d. Emission of fly ash during electricity generation.

Correct answer(s):

     a. Alteration of natural river flow patterns.
      b. Disruption of fish migration and aquatic ecosystems.
      c. Submergence of terrestrial habitats due to reservoir formation.
Solution: Refer to Slide #16 of Lecture #4. Large hydroelectric dams can modify natural
river flow regimes, impede fish migration, and inundate terrestrial ecosystems through

QUESTION 13:

Which of the following statements about tidal energy are correct?

      a. It is driven primarily by the gravitational interaction of the Earth, Moon, and Sun.
      b. It is one of the most predictable renewable energy resources.
      c. Tidal barrages utilize differences in water level across a basin.
      d. The magnitude of tidal currents and tidal ranges is largely independent of local

          coastal geography.

Correct answer(s):

     a. It is driven primarily by the gravitational interaction of the Earth, Moon, and
          Sun.

      b. It is one of the most predictable renewable energy resources.
      c. Tidal barrages utilize differences in water level across a basin.
Solution: Refer to Slide #6 of Lecture #2. Tidal energy is governed by gravitational forces

coastal geometry strongly influences tidal ranges and current velocities.

QUESTION 15:

Which of the following are recognized environmental benefits associated with the
production and use of biodiesel?

      a. Reduced reliance on petroleum-based fuels.
      b. Complete elimination of exhaust emissions from diesel engines.
      c. Reduced environmental persistence in the event of accidental spills.
      d. Valorization of waste oils and animal fats as feedstocks.

Correct answer(s):

      a. Reduced reliance on petroleum-based fuels.
      c. Reduced environmental persistence in the event of accidental spills.
      d. Valorization of waste oils and animal fats as feedstocks.
Solution: Biodiesel can reduce dependence on petroleum fuels, is generally more
biodegradable in the event of accidental spills, and can be produced from waste oils and
animal fats. However, biodiesel combustion still generates exhaust emissions and does
not eliminate them completely.
