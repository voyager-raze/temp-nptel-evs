50 percent is available at the autotrophic level. I have already mentioned two parameters
related to productivity. Productivity is measured in terms of gross primary productivity and
net primary productivity. So, GPP is equal to NPP plus R. GPP is the total amount of energy
consumed per unit area per unit time. So, we have units of kilocalories per square meter per

respiration. Respiration is the energy consumed in metabolism and other activities without
generating biomass. You can think of a plant growing, it is when it is growing it is adding
biomass. So, that addition of biomass is what we are counting as net primary productivity. If
you were to pull up that plant and measure the weight of the plant you would find that the
weight has increased. So, that is the increase in biomass and therefore, that is called net
primary productivity. That is the chemical energy that is locked in the biomass and that is
what we eat when we eat food or vegetation and so on that is what we are getting is the
chemical energy that is locked in the biomass. It is also called the standing crop or the
biomass of photosynthetic organisms. What is respiration? Respiration in plants happens
