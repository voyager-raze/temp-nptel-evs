Coliforms are bacteria that are defined as rod-shaped, gram-negative, non-spore forming,
motile meaning they are capable of moving motile or non-motile bacteria which can
ferment lactose and produce acid and gas when they are incubated at 35 to 37 degree
centigrade. Why are we focusing on coliforms? These coliform bacteria are considered to
be indicator organisms because they are normal inhabitants of the gastrointestinal tract of
warm-blooded animals which includes human beings. So, human beings, birds, the
mammals around you, dogs, cats, and so on. These are all warm-blooded animals and the
gastrointestinal tract of these animals has tons and tons of coliform bacteria.

They are non-pathogenic. They are required, they are essential for our ability to digest
complex food. So, they are definitely a requirement for our ability to digest food. However,
if these bacteria are present in either drinking water or any other water body, they are
representative of fecal contamination. So, when you think about municipal sewage,
municipal sewage is basically fecal matter that is being discharged and it is going to have
very high numbers of coliforms and this will become again very important when we look
at water body classification as well as drinking water treatment.
