Before I go into the methodology that was adopted for this particular project, it is important
for you as a student to know what is the methodology followed for an EIA. So, this just
gives you the basic steps that need to be followed in any environmental impact assessment.
So, the first step is screening. Does the proposed project require an EIA? Is it big enough
to require an EIA? And generally, these large infrastructure projects without question
require an EIA. The second question is what is the level of assessment? And in a project
like this, a fairly detailed assessment is required. The second step is coping. What are the
issues? What are the impacts? What is going to be the boundary of the EIA? What are the
timelines for the project as well as for the impacts? All these things, once the answer in the
first step is yes, then in the second step we have all these issues to be defined. The third
step is to actually conduct the impact analysis. What are the potential environmental as
well as social impacts? Those have to be identified. One has to come up with design
alternatives. It's not sufficient to come up with a single proposal. You have to come up with
certain alternatives. You have to say if this is not good, then let me try another way and
another way and so on to achieve the same objectives. So, remember our objectives are
providing water, municipal water supply, industrial water supply and irrigation water
supply. Is there any other source of water? What are the other sources of water? Can they
be better? So those kinds of questions have to be answered. Design alternatives are
proposed and compared. The alternative which has the least cost and cost is not only
financial cost, but it includes environmental cost as well. So least cost and highest benefit
is normally chosen as the final alternative. Then we come to mitigation. Now let us say out
of the given alternatives, you choose your best alternative, but there are still certain
problems associated with it. So those require mitigation. Actions to reduce or avoid
potential adverse environmental consequences of the development activities have to be
recommended. That's part of the EIA. In fact, that is the literally the bottom line for the
person who's doing the EIA. This is the most important point. And then finally, you have
to prepare a report and present it to the decision makers and other stakeholders. So, you
can imagine that the stakeholders in this kind of project would be the municipalities of the
three urban areas, the urban local bodies. It would be the farmers in this region and it would
be the industries in this region. So, these are the stakeholders along with the administrative
authorities. Then we come to review of EIA. Is the EIA adequate and effective in terms of
the report? So, the report will be examined by the decision makers. They will determine
whether the report is adequate and it answers all the questions that are required to make a
decision. Finally, the decision is made by the authorities to either reject, approve or modify
the proposal. After the proposal is accepted, there has to be a post monitoring of the
impacts. So, project impacts have to be checked to ensure that legal standards are met.
Mitigation measures are implemented as described in the report. So, it's an eight-step
process and what I'm going to cover for obvious reasons are the first five steps.

have to be resettled in some other areas and vegetation is basically your forested areas or
lower vegetation as well. So, if that vegetation is lost that is also useful land area that is
lost. So, this useful land area is a major loss whenever you have a dam or a barrage project.
So, there is a conflict between these two objectives, satisfying water demand and loss of
useful land. So, finding a compromise between these two conflicting objectives is always
very difficult. The alternative that was finally proposed was 4.5 m which satisfies 8.25 %
of the total annual demand and submerges 770 hectares of useful land.
How can we mitigate these impacts? So, submergence like I said is a major issue. There
