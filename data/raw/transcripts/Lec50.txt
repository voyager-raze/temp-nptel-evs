 So, when we talk about noise control we have 3 points at which noise can be controlled.
So, we have our source the path that the sound takes and the receiver. Now we can control
noise at any one or more of these points. So, I will start with source control. What is the
source of noise? Most of the time the source of noise is impact.

 So, you have moving parts within a machine within an engine and these moving parts
may be colliding with each other and generating noise. So, you have to reduce the impact
of these moving parts and providing shock absorbers, mufflers, lubrication all these are
methods for reducing the amount of noise generated by impact forces. That is the first
method. Second method is to reduce the speed of the moving parts. If noise is being
generated because of high pressure, high flow velocities which are causing turbulence and
that is adding to the noise that can also be reduced. That is another method. A lot of noise
is generated by machines because of frictional resistance. So, you can improve the
alignment, the balance, the polishing and the eccentricity of the gears and the machines
and whatever else can be done to reduce frictional forces will help to reduce noise. We
can reduce the surface area of the machine that is transmitting or radiating noise. So, if it
is smaller or if you can reduce the area that is exposed that will also help to reduce noise.

 Now noise leakage happens because of open joints within the entire, no within the entire
covering of the machine or the engine and so on. So, by providing appropriate insulation
and or caulking, insulation is often provided with rubber gaskets to make the joints airtight
and all these methods or even providing a cover for a moving engine or a moving part, all
these methods are used to reduce noise leakage from the moving parts or from the machine
engine whatever it is that is generating noise. I have already mentioned silencers, mufflers
and shock absorbers. These are common methods that are used, devices and methods that
are used especially in motor vehicles, these include motorbikes, cars and so on. They all
have these kinds of devices for reducing the amount of noise generated.
You can reduce vibrations in a machine by providing either a cover and these covers can
be made out of soft material that can absorb the vibrations and therefore reduce the noise.
That is one method and the second method is to anchor the machine properly so that the
vibrations are reduced and then you provide a cover as well so that will in many cases
eliminate noise. We will then be looking at path control and receiver control in subsequent
slides.

activity levels are less than 55 dB and for hearing loss, it is 70 dB.
